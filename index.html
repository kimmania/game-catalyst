How to Play

Pouring

Tap a source beaker, then tap a destination beaker to pour its top color on top.

Colors stack regardless of what lies underneath. Matching a different primary color on top triggers a reaction.

Reactions

The primaries are crimson, amber, viridian, cobalt, and saffron. When two different primaries meet, they fuse into one intermediate color.

Watch the Discovered Reactions panel below the board for the pairs you have already seen.

Catalyst

Select a beaker with an intermediate color on top, then press Catalyst to split it back into its two parent colors. Charges are limited.

Solidification

Pouring two of the same primary layer together fuses them into a permanent crystal at the bottom, permanently reducing that beaker's space.

Solve within the target moves for 3 stars!

Goal

Every non-empty beaker must contain one uniform color (any color, primary or intermediate). Empty beakers are fine.

Beat a level using no catalyst and no solidification to earn all three stars.
