How to Play

Pouring

Tap a source beaker, then tap a destination beaker to pour.

Colors stack on top of each other regardless of what's underneath. Pairing two different primary colors triggers a reaction.

Reactions

When two adjacent primary colors come into contact, they fuse into a single intermediate layer. The reaction table shows every valid pair.

Catalyst Charges

Tap a beaker with an intermediate on top, then press Catalyst to split it back into its two parent colors. Charges are limited — use them wisely.

Solidification

Pouring the same primary color onto itself fuses consecutive matching layers into permanent crystals at the bottom, reducing that beaker's capacity.

Avoid solidification for a perfect solve!

Goal

Every non-empty beaker must contain one uniform color (any color, primary or intermediate). Empty beakers are fine.

A perfect solve uses zero solidifications and zero catalysts.
